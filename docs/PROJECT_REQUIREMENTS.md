## Version

1.0

---

# Project Overview

Shine On Workshop ERP is a mobile-first workshop management system designed for automobile service centers.

The system helps manage customers, vehicles, jobs, payments, expenses, inventory, employees, reports and overall business finances from one application.

The primary objective is to give workshop owners complete visibility into every vehicle, every job and every rupee entering or leaving the business.

---

# Problem Statement

Currently the workshop operates manually.

Major problems include:

- Customer advances are mixed with other cash.
- Difficult to know where money was spent.
- No job profitability tracking.
- No customer history.
- No vehicle history.
- Difficult to manage employees.
- Inventory is managed manually.
- Daily revenue and expenses are not easily visible.
- Reports take time to prepare.

---

# Project Goals

The application should:

- Digitize workshop operations.
- Track every customer.
- Track every vehicle.
- Track every workshop job.
- Track payments.
- Track expenses.
- Calculate profit automatically.
- Manage inventory.
- Generate reports.
- Improve business decisions using analytics.

# User Roles

## Owner

Full access.

Can access every module.

---

## Manager

Can manage daily workshop operations.

Cannot modify system settings.

---

## Receptionist

Can create customers.

Can register vehicles.

Can create jobs.

Can receive payments.

---

## Employee

Can only view assigned jobs.

Can update job progress.

Can upload work photos.
